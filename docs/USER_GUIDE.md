## Getting Started

### Welcome to The Great Beans

The Great Beans is a comprehensive B2B platform for Vietnamese coffee exports, designed to connect global importers, roasters, and distributors with premium coffee suppliers. This guide will help you navigate and utilize all platform features effectively.

### First-Time Users

1. **Browse Products**: Start by exploring our [product catalog](/products) to understand our coffee offerings
2. **Request Quotes**: Use our streamlined quote request system for pricing information
3. **Create Account**: Register for enhanced features and dashboard access
4. **Contact Support**: Reach out via our [contact page](/contact) for assistance

## Quote Request System

### Request for Quote (RFQ) Process

#### Step 1: Product Selection

- Choose from our product catalog
- Specify product variants and grades
- Select required certifications

#### Step 2: Quantity & Specifications

- Enter desired quantity (minimum order quantities apply)
- Choose packaging options:
  - Jute bags (60kg, 69kg)
  - Bulk containers
  - Custom packaging
- Specify quality requirements

#### Step 3: Shipping & Delivery

- Select shipping method:
  - FOB (Free on Board)
  - CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)
  - Custom Incoterms
- Provide destination port/address
- Set preferred delivery timeline

#### Step 4: Contact Information

- Company details and registration
- Contact person information
- Special requirements or notes

### Quote Management

Access your quotes through:

- **Direct Links**: Emailed quote references
- **Dashboard**: Comprehensive quote tracking (requires account)
- **RFQ Pages**: Individual quote status pages (`/rfq/[id]`)

#### Quote Status Tracking

- **Pending**: Quote request received, under review
- **Quoted**: Pricing provided, awaiting response
- **Approved**: Quote accepted, processing order
- **Shipped**: Order dispatched
- **Delivered**: Order completed

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Problems

- **Forgot Password**: Use password reset functionality
- **Account Locked**: Contact support for assistance
- **Email Verification**: Check spam folder for verification emails
- **Two-Factor Issues**: Use backup codes or contact support

#### Quote Request Issues

- **Form Errors**: Ensure all required fields are completed
- **File Upload Problems**: Check file size and format requirements
- **Submission Failures**: Verify internet connection and retry
- **Missing Quotes**: Check email spam folder and dashboard

#### Product Browsing Issues

- **Images Not Loading**: Clear browser cache and refresh
- **Filter Problems**: Reset filters and try again
- **Search Issues**: Use different keywords or contact support
- **Comparison Tool**: Ensure JavaScript is enabled

#### Dashboard Access

- **Loading Problems**: Clear browser cache and cookies
- **Missing Data**: Refresh page or contact support
- **Permission Errors**: Verify account permissions with admin
- **Export Issues**: Check browser popup blockers
