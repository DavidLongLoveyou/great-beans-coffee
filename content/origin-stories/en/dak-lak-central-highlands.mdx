# Dak Lak Central Highlands: Vietnam's Coffee Capital

In the heart of Vietnam's Central Highlands, Dak Lak Province stands as the undisputed capital of Vietnamese coffee production. This legendary region, centered around the bustling city of Buon Ma Thuot, produces nearly 60% of Vietnam's coffee output and has earned global recognition for its exceptional Robusta beans and increasingly impressive Arabica varieties.
